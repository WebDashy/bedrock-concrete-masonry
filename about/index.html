About Us

Founded in {{ site.business.founded }}, Bedrock Concrete & Masonry was built on one rule: pour to the engineered spec and document it.

Our story

Bedrock started with a simple complaint from local GCs: too many concrete subs showed up without the mix ticket, skipped cure time to hit a schedule, and left the GC holding the bag at inspection. So Bedrock built its whole operation around the paperwork nobody else wanted to bother with — mix designs, slump tests, cure logs, inspection sign-offs — all handed over as a matter of course, not a special request.

Today Bedrock pours foundations, walls, decorative flatwork, and commercial paving side by side, with the same in-house crew and the same documentation standard on every job, residential or commercial.
