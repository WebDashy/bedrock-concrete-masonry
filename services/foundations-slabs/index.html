Services / Foundations & Slabs

The part of the building nobody sees, poured right the first time

A foundation that's off-spec by even a fraction of an inch causes problems for the life of the building. We pour to the engineered drawing, not "close enough."

Get an Estimate

What's included

  • Excavation and forming to the engineered foundation plan, checked before any concrete arrives
  • Rebar and reinforcement placed and inspected to spec, with photos on file
  • Mix design matched to soil conditions and load requirements — not a generic bag mix
  • Slump testing and mix tickets provided for every truck, every pour
  • Proper cure time and moisture control, no shortcuts to hit a schedule
  • Coordination with your structural engineer through final inspection sign-off

Typical timeline

A standard residential foundation typically runs 1–2 weeks from excavation to cured slab, including a minimum 7-day cure before framing begins. Commercial foundations vary by size and structural requirements — we'll give you a pour-by-pour schedule with your quote.

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide mix tickets and inspection documentation?

On every pour, as a matter of course — you'll have them before the inspector asks.

Can you coordinate directly with our structural engineer?

Yes — we regularly work from engineered drawings and coordinate rebar placement and mix design directly with the engineer of record.

What happens if soil conditions differ from the geotech report?

We stop and flag it before pouring. Adjusting the foundation design after concrete is in the ground is far more expensive than a short delay before.
