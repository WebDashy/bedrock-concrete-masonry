Services / Stamped & Decorative Concrete

Finish work that holds its color and its pattern for years

Decorative concrete lives or dies on timing — sealing, coloring, and stamping have narrow windows in the cure process. We don't rush them.

What's included

  • Pattern and color selection with physical samples, not just a catalog photo
  • Integral color or color hardener mixed and applied to spec, not eyeballed
  • Stamped pattern work timed precisely to the concrete's set — too early or late ruins the texture permanently
  • Exposed aggregate finishing for a natural stone look at a fraction of the cost
  • Control joint layout planned to minimize visible cracking as concrete settles
  • Sealer application and a maintenance schedule to keep color and finish looking new

Typical timeline

A standard patio or walkway of 300–800 sq ft typically runs 1–2 weeks including forming, pouring, stamping, and sealing. Driveways and larger installations run 2–3 weeks depending on square footage and pattern complexity.

Frequently asked questions

How long before we can walk or drive on it?

Foot traffic after about 24–48 hours; vehicle traffic on driveways after a full 7-day cure. We'll give you exact timing based on weather and mix at the time of your pour.

Will the color fade over time?

All decorative concrete needs periodic resealing — typically every 2–3 years in Colorado's UV exposure — to keep color from fading. We'll walk you through the maintenance schedule at handoff.

Can you match an existing stamped pattern on our property?

In most cases yes — we carry a wide stamp library and can source additional patterns to match existing work.
