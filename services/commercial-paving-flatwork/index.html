Services / Commercial Paving & Flatwork

Flatwork sized for daily truck traffic, not just opening day

A parking lot or loading dock that looks right at handover but wasn't sized for real load cycles fails within a few years. We spec ours for the traffic you'll actually run.

Get an Estimate

What's included

  • Subgrade and base preparation sized to your actual traffic loads — passenger vehicles, delivery trucks, or forklifts
  • Slab thickness and reinforcement engineered for daily commercial use, not residential-grade flatwork
  • Drainage and grading designed to keep water off the slab and away from building foundations
  • Joint layout planned to control cracking under thermal and load stress
  • Phased pouring available to keep part of your lot operational during construction
  • ADA-compliant ramps, striping layout coordination, and curb work as needed

Typical timeline

A standard commercial parking area of 10,000–20,000 sq ft typically runs 3–5 weeks from base prep to cured, striped surface. Loading dock and apron work with heavier reinforcement runs 4–6 weeks. We'll build a phased schedule around your operating hours where needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can you pour in phases so our business stays open?

Yes — this is one of our most common commercial requests. We'll section the work so part of your lot or dock stays operational throughout.

Do you coordinate with our striping and signage contractor?

We can either coordinate with your existing vendor or handle striping ourselves — either way, joint and layout planning accounts for it from the start.

What's the difference between commercial and residential flatwork?

Primarily slab thickness, base depth, and reinforcement — commercial flatwork is engineered for repeated heavy loads that residential driveways never see.
