What's included

  • Soil and drainage assessment before design — most wall failures are drainage failures, not structural ones
  • Engineered wall design sized to actual retained height and soil load, with permitting where required
  • Proper drainage systems built behind every wall — gravel backfill, drain tile, and weep holes
  • Segmental block, poured concrete, brick, or natural stone finish options
  • Footing depth and reinforcement to local frost-line requirements
  • Final grading and compaction so the wall performs as designed from day one

Typical timeline

A standard residential retaining wall of 50–150 linear feet typically runs 2–4 weeks from excavation to finished grading. Taller engineered walls requiring permits and structural review run 4–8 weeks including design and approval time.

Frequently asked questions

Does my retaining wall need a permit?

In most Denver metro jurisdictions, walls over 4 feet in height require engineering and a permit. We handle that process as part of every wall over that threshold.

Why do so many retaining walls fail?

Almost always drainage — water pressure builds up behind a wall with no way to escape. We build drainage into every wall regardless of size, not just the ones that require it by code.

Can you match masonry to an existing wall or building?

Yes — we source block, brick, and stone to match existing structures where a close match exists, and we'll tell you up front if it doesn't.
